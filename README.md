# Overlay Map: Outlines

The pictures for [Overlay Map](https://github.com/benja2030/Overlay-Map-WoW-Forever)'s **Outlines** style: the map's painting hidden, and only white lines for cliffs, water and roads over a dark background, like the overlay maps of other games. A separate download, as the pictures are much bigger than Overlay Map itself. Install it next to Overlay Map; it's loaded only when the style is chosen.

What's in it: one picture per zone of Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms (`Outlines/<map ID>.blp`), made from the game's own terrain: where the ground gets too steep to walk, the edges of the water, and the roads, from the textures the game paints them with.

Also the lines of 287 caves, mines and underground places (`Caves/`, listed in `CaveList.lua`), shown inside them with OpenMap: their walls and water, from the game's minimap pictures of each cave (for the caves with a Blizzard cave map, the edge of its map, from OpenMap's cave masks, MIT licence).

# Overlay Map: Outlines (español)

Las imágenes del estilo **Outlines** de Overlay Map: la pintura del mapa oculta, y solo líneas blancas de acantilados, agua y caminos sobre un fondo oscuro. Es una descarga aparte, porque las imágenes pesan mucho más que el propio Overlay Map. Instálalo junto a Overlay Map; solo se carga cuando eliges ese estilo.

Incluye también las líneas de 287 cuevas, minas y lugares subterráneos, que se ven dentro de ellas con OpenMap.
