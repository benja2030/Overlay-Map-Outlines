# Changelog

## 0.3.0-beta (2026-11-21)

- Caves: lines for 287 caves, mines and underground places, shown inside them with OpenMap (Overlay Map 1.2.2 or later). Their walls and water, from the pictures the game's minimap draws each cave with; for the caves with a Blizzard cave map, the edge of its map (from OpenMap's cave masks, MIT licence).

## 0.2.1-beta (2026-11-20)

- Elwynn Forest: a road by Forest's Edge that doesn't exist (a patch of dirt joined to the road) is gone.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-20)

- Roads traced from the painted maps of Arathi Highlands, Badlands, Thousand Needles, Stonetalon Mountains, Dustwallow Marsh and Azshara, where the game's road textures only showed part of them. Broken, dashed and dotted roads joined into one line.

## 0.1.9 (2026-11-20)

- Silithus' roads traced from its painted map: the road from Ahn'Qiraj's gates past Cenarion Hold to Un'Goro.

## 0.1.8 (2026-11-20)

- Desolace's roads traced from its painted map, and the traced roads drawn cleaner (no stray joins between leftover marks).

## 0.1.7 (2026-11-20)

- Dun Morogh's and Winterspring's roads traced from their painted maps: the road through Kharanos and the loop past Brewnall, and Winterspring's from Frostsaber Rock through Everlook.

## 0.1.6 (2026-11-20)

- Roads painted as footprints in sand and snow (Tanaris, Winterspring) are drawn. Where those textures cover wide ground instead of tracing roads (Dun Morogh, Desolace), they're left out, as before.

## 0.1.5 (2026-11-20)

- More of the small ridges (from about 60 yards long), drawn fainter than the rest, so the roads, water and mountains stand out first.

## 0.1.4 (2026-11-20)

- More of the narrow cliffs: read from the unsmoothed heights, and the pieces of one ridge joined, so the ridges across open ground (south of Goldshire, say) show as lines.

## 0.1.3 (2026-11-20)

- Narrow cliffs: steep drops too thin to count as mountains (the ridges the map paints across open ground, Westfall's coastal cliffs) are drawn as a single line along them, as they block the way.

## 0.1.2 (2026-11-20)

- More of each road network: roads painted very lightly are kept, and a road's loose end is joined to the next piece of road it points at (bridges, fords, stretches painted too lightly), so roads aren't cut where they cross rivers.

## 0.1.1 (2026-11-20)

- More roads: the lighter-painted ones are kept, and Elwynn Forest's main road is whole again.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-20)

- First test version: the pictures for Overlay Map's Outlines style, one per zone of Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms.
